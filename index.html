How to play

A mother dragon is asleep next to her nest. You sneak in to warm her egg until it hatches. But every roll might wake her up!

Step 1: Find your egg

Roll both dice and add them. That total is your egg number. If you roll a 7 first, you found a Golden Egg: score 5 points and your turn is over.

Step 2: Warm the egg

Keep rolling. Roll your egg number and the egg hatches: score your warmth plus the hatching bonus. Roll a 7 and the dragon wakes up: you lose all your warmth. Any other roll adds 1 warmth.

Tiptoe away

Before any roll, you can sneak out and keep half your warmth (round down). The egg and the other half stay behind. The next player can adopt that egg or find their own.

Winning

When someone reaches the target score, finish the round so everyone has had the same number of turns. Most points wins. A tie means one more turn each.
